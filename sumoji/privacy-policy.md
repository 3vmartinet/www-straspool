# Privacy Policy for SUMOJI

**Last Updated:** 30.08.2025

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled in the mobile game application SUMOJI ("the Game"), developed by Valentin Martinet ("we," "our," or "us").

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and why, solely for the purpose of providing, improving, and securing the Game's functionality.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

*   **Device Identifier:** We collect a unique identifier associated with your device (such as an Android ID or Advertising Identifier on iOS). This identifier does not reveal your personal identity (like your name or email address) but is unique to your specific device.
*   **Analytics and Performance Data:** We collect anonymous, aggregated data about how users interact with the Game. This includes information on game features used, session length, and performance metrics (such as crash reports and latency). This data is not linked to your specific device identifier and cannot be used to identify you personally.

## 2. How We Use This Information

*   **Device Identifier:** This identifier is used to associate your game saves, high scores, and unlocked achievements directly with your device. This allows you to close the app and return later without losing your progress.
*   **Analytics and Performance Data:** We use this anonymous information to:
    *   Understand user behavior and preferences to improve gameplay and design.
    *   Identify and fix bugs, crashes, and other technical issues to ensure the Game runs smoothly and reliably.

## 3. What We Do NOT Do

We are committed to your privacy and therefore:

*   **We do not track your activity** across other applications or websites.
*   **We do not collect or store** personal information like your name, email address, or physical address.
*   **We do not link** our analytics data to your device identifier or any other identifying information.
*   **We do not share** or sell your device identifier or any other data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Your game progress (scores and achievements) linked to your device identifier is stored securely. We take reasonable precautions to protect this information, but remember that no method of electronic storage over the internet is 100% secure.

## 5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Your game progress is stored in association with your device identifier. Simply uninstalling the Game may not delete this data from our systems.

If you wish to request the deletion of all data associated with your device, please contact us at straspool+sumoji@gmail.com. To process your request, we will need to identify your data. Please include the following in your email:
*   **Your User ID:** You can find this unique identifier within the Game's settings menu.
*   **The name of the Game:** "SUMOJI".

Upon receiving your request with this information, we will promptly delete all your associated data from our servers.

## 6. Children's Privacy

SUMOJI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us, and we will take steps to delete such information.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
